# First use and account checks

[简体中文](../claude-first-run.md) | **English** · [Guide home](../../README.en.md)

Complete network verification before visiting [Claude](https://claude.ai/). Follow the current official requirements for your actual location. An overseas exit does not change eligibility.

## Google account region

Sign into the account you want to check and open [Google's country association page](https://policies.google.com/country-association-form). Read the current region stated in the page text. The form below requests a change; it is not the current result, and viewing your region does not require submission.

Account association, Google Play country, payment profile, current IP, and actual location are different facts. None guarantees Claude registration or exemption from verification.

## Login and privacy

Use your own account through official pages. Enter passwords, verification codes, and identity documents yourself. The welcome page may offer model-improvement preferences; choose according to your privacy needs and the current explanation. This is not an account-risk workaround.

If an account is on hold, read the stated date and reason. The hold may predate the current network setup. Request an official review with truthful information. Switching accounts, resetting device identifiers, or buying verified accounts is not a recovery procedure.

## Timezone

Change the system timezone only when requested. UTC+8 is eight hours ahead of UTC. Beijing, Singapore, and Taipei generally show the same clock time with different zone names. Los Angeles uses daylight saving time, so do not hard-code a permanent fifteen-hour difference.

Websites may read language, timezone, and device details. These are not themselves IP leaks, and this project has no verified “ban-proof timezone.” Use official subscription, payment, and verification processes; do not fabricate billing details or rotate accounts to evade limits. Social-media account-aging advice is not an official requirement.
