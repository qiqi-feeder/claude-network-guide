## DMIT availability: plan for a wait

**Popular low-cost and limited promotional plans can be hard to get.** Plan for possible restocks rather than assuming your preferred offer will always be available. This is purchasing guidance, not a measured restock pattern or a claim that every DMIT plan is sold out.

The official page showed both `Order Now` and `Out of Stock` when checked. Follow official stock information and announcements. If you need a server immediately, compare available standard plans or BandwagonHost instead of interrupting a working setup while waiting for a promotion. No restock date is promised here; buying someone else's account is not recommended.

## Before you order

- For a US server accessed from mainland China, compare BandwagonHost CN2 GIA and DMIT Premium using your ISP, peak-hour packet loss, and budget. A higher price does not guarantee better performance on your connection.
- Start with an entry plan for light personal use; upgrade for actual concurrency and transfer needs.
- Check dedicated public IPv4, OS support, SSH access, transfer accounting, and refund conditions. A hosting provider, datacenter IP, or risk score cannot guarantee account access or service eligibility. “Residential,” “native,” and “clean” are different claims and may be marketing terms.
